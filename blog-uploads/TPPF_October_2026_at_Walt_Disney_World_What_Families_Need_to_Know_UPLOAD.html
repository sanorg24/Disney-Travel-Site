October 2026 at Walt Disney World

October at Walt Disney World brings Halloween parties, Food & Wine, cooler evenings, race-weekend changes and plenty of reasons to slow down and check the calendar before you head out the door. Here are the things we would want to know before an October trip — especially the details that can change how you plan a park day.

Magic Kingdom Closes at 6 PM on 16 Nights in October

Mickey’s Not-So-Scary Halloween Party takes place on 16 nights in October, and all remaining 2026 party dates are sold out.

October 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 9, 13, 15, 16, 18, 22, 23, 25, 27, 29 and 31.

If Magic Kingdom is on your October plans, check the calendar first. There are 16 nights this month when the park closes to regular daytime guests at 6 PM for Mickey’s Not-So-Scary Halloween Party.

But that doesn’t necessarily make them bad Magic Kingdom days — especially if you have Park Hopper tickets. Party dates can sometimes bring lighter daytime crowds, making them a great option to rope drop Magic Kingdom, enjoy the park earlier in the day, and hop somewhere else before the party begins.

The tradeoff? You give up your regular Magic Kingdom evening. So if nighttime rides and fireworks are important to your family, choose a non-party date instead.

Wine & Dine Weekend Can Change More Than a Runner’s Morning

The Disney Wine & Dine Half Marathon Weekend runs October 22–25. Even if nobody in your family is running, this is worth knowing before you make transportation or EPCOT plans.

The 5K is Friday, October 23, the 10K is Saturday, October 24, and the Half Marathon is Sunday, October 25. Race operations begin very early, with road closures affecting areas around EPCOT, World Drive, Buena Vista Drive and other resort roads. On the 10K and Half Marathon mornings especially, build in extra transportation time instead of assuming your normal Disney travel time will work.

One very specific example: Winter Summerland Miniature Golf is scheduled to delay opening until 11:30 AM on Sunday, October 25 because of the Half Marathon. That is a good reminder that race weekend can affect plans outside the race itself.

EPCOT Opens Later on Two Race-Weekend Dates

EPCOT’s currently published schedule shows a later opening on Friday, October 23 and Sunday, October 25: Early Entry is scheduled for 9:30 AM and regular park opening for 10 AM, one hour later than the usual October schedule.

That matters if your family normally plans breakfast, transportation or a rope-drop strategy around an earlier EPCOT start. Sunday also brings the Wine & Dine Post-Race Party at EPCOT beginning at 10 PM, with party ticket holders allowed to enter as early as 5 PM.

Disney can adjust operating hours, so this is one of those dates we would check again shortly before the trip.

The Walt Disney World Railroad Is Down for Refurbishment

The Walt Disney World Railroad temporarily closed beginning September 28 for routine refurbishment and Disney currently expects it to reopen sometime in November 2026. There is no exact reopening date posted yet.

For some families, this is more than losing an attraction. The train is also a relaxing way to get off your feet and move between areas of Magic Kingdom. If you normally count on that break — especially with kids, older family members or anyone who needs more pacing built into the day — plan for a little more walking and a different rest strategy.

Blizzard Beach Is the Water Park for October

Typhoon Lagoon is temporarily closed for refurbishment, and Disney is directing guests to Blizzard Beach during the closure. Blizzard Beach is currently operating in October, with hours that can vary by date.

If a water-park day is part of your vacation, don’t arrive assuming both parks are available. Check the current calendar before you go — especially because weather and operating schedules can change.

Food & Wine Is in Full Swing — and October Just Added More

The EPCOT International Food & Wine Festival continues through November 21, so October visitors get the full festival atmosphere throughout the month. Beginning October 2, four additional Global Marketplaces join the festival: Coastal Eats, Earth Eats, India and The Alps.

But when you visit the festival matters, too.

Friday and Saturday evenings can get especially busy. EPCOT festivals are a favorite hangout for local Annual Passholders, and once Florida finally starts giving us a little break from the summer heat, we’re ready to come out of summer hibernation too. 😂 Add a popular Eat to the Beat performer, and World Showcase can get very busy.

Most festival marketplaces typically begin serving around 11 AM, so the lunch rush can build quickly around noon as well.

What works best for our family? If the food booths are a priority, we like to get there right as they begin opening and beat the lunch crowds. Our other favorite window is mid-afternoon — after lunch but before the dinner and evening crowds arrive.

If you’re visiting on a Friday or Saturday, we’d be especially intentional about timing. Do your must-try booths earlier and save the busiest evening hours for enjoying the festival atmosphere, entertainment or things that don’t require standing in another long food line.

A Little Trick We Use When Looking at October Crowds

Disney doesn’t publish an official crowd calendar, but here’s one little clue we sometimes look at when we’re choosing park days: Lightning Lane Multi Pass pricing.

Disney uses date-based pricing for Lightning Lane Multi Pass, and as dates enter the advance-purchase window, you can look across the calendar and see what Disney is charging for different days.

We don’t treat those prices as a crowd forecast — because they aren’t one. But when we’re deciding between dates, they’re another useful piece of the puzzle.

Take October at Magic Kingdom. Current Multi Pass pricing starts at $27 on October 2 and reaches $39 on several later dates. That’s a pretty noticeable difference.

We still consider weekends, parties, festivals, school breaks and special events, but we’ll absolutely peek at Lightning Lane pricing too.

TPPF TIP: Don’t use Lightning Lane pricing to predict exactly how crowded a park will be. Use it as one more clue when you have flexibility and are deciding between two or three possible park days.

Disney Springs Is Worth an October Evening

Disney Springs has plenty of fall and Halloween energy without requiring another theme-park ticket. Disney’s current fall lineup includes seasonal food and sweets, fall merchandise and entertainment such as the Zombeatz Drummers, Spooky Stilt Walkers and the Roving Pumpkin DJ. Jock Lindsey’s Hangar Bar is also in its seasonal Halloween transformation through November 9, with themed food, drinks and décor.

You can get some of the fall atmosphere without committing to another full park day. Grab dinner, wander through the decorations, catch some entertainment and do a little shopping — including a stop at World of Disney, home to the largest collection of Disney merchandise in the world.

For families who have already packed several park days into a vacation, sometimes that kind of night is exactly what you need.

Check Your Resort Rate Before Your October Trip

Disney currently has a general-public room offer for select Disney Resorts Collection rooms, with savings of up to 20% on qualifying stays beginning October 4. For the October portion of the offer, the published savings apply to most Sunday-through-Thursday nights from October 4 through November 19, with availability and resort exclusions.

Annual Passholders also have a separate holiday room offer with savings of up to 30% on select rooms and qualifying nights beginning October 4.

If you already have a Disney Resort reservation, it is worth checking whether an eligible offer can be applied to your stay. Disney specifically provides a way to check existing reservations for available vacation offers. Availability is limited, so a discount existing does not mean every resort, room type or date will qualify.

Give Yourself a Little Room in the Plan

October is one of those Walt Disney World months where a few calendar details can make a big difference. A 6 PM Magic Kingdom closing can become a great park-hopping strategy. A race weekend can change your morning transportation. And the right Food & Wine timing can be the difference between walking up to a booth and staring at a line stretching down World Showcase.

Know the dates that affect your family, leave a little flexibility, and then go enjoy the trip. You don’t have to schedule every minute to have a plan that works.

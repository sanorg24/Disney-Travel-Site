If you're visiting Magic Kingdom this fall, there's a new limited-time dining experience worth knowing about—especially if you like a little flexibility in your park-day meal plans.

\*\*The Diamond Horseshoe pop-up begins October 4, 2026\*\*, with dining reservations opening \*\*September 29\*\*.

The experience will feature a frontier-inspired lineup of small plates, specialty nonalcoholic drinks and cocktails inside the Old West-style restaurant in Liberty Square.

## What's on the Menu

![Charcuterie platter with candied bacon, cured meats, cheeses and pickles from The Diamond Horseshoe pop-up menu.](/photos/20260908-150642.png "Shareable bites at The Diamond Horseshoe")

The menu focuses on shareable bites rather than a traditional full meal.

Disney has announced options including \*\*Charcuterie, Glazed Sticky Ribs, Queso Fundido with Barbecue Burnt Ends, Sweet Golden Corn Fritters, Chipotle Chicken Tostada\*\* and a \*\*Mini Hot Dog Platter\*\*.

There are also nonalcoholic specialty drinks such as \*\*Orange Cream Soda, Caramel Root Beer with Sweet Cream, Enchanting Ember Lemonade\*\* and \*\*Enchanted Grove Lime Splash\*\*, along with cocktails, wine and draft beer.

For families, the shareable format could make this a nice option if you want to sit down, cool off and try a few different things without committing to a large traditional meal. 

What I like about this one is the flexibility. On a long Magic Kingdom day, having a place to sit down, cool off and share a few things can feel a lot more appealing than committing to a big meal when everyone just needs a break.

## One Date You'll Want to Remember

If this sounds like something you'd like to try, \*\*September 29 is the reservation date to remember\*\*.

The experience begins October 4, but Disney has already confirmed that the pop-up \*\*will not be available during Mickey's Not-So-Scary Halloween Party\*\*.

It \*\*will be available during Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party\*\*, so holiday-party guests may have another dining option to consider. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

As with any limited-time Disney dining offering, menus, availability and other details can change. Check [Disney's official Diamond Horseshoe page](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/en_CA/dining/magic-kingdom/diamond-horseshoe-lounge/) before your visit for the latest information.

If Tiana's Bayou Adventure is on your Magic Kingdom list, this is worth knowing before your trip.

According to reporting from DFB Guide and WDW News Today, the ride is expected to close for refurbishment beginning **November 2, 2026**. Disney has not announced an official reopening date. December 2026 has been mentioned in early reporting, but no specific date has been confirmed — treat that window as a possibility, not a promise.

If you're visiting around that timeframe, keep an eye on Disney's official attraction information as your trip gets closer.

## Why This One Is Special to Us

The Princess and the Frog has always been a favorite for Arianna and me, so we watched this one come together with a little more excitement than usual. We counted down through the construction and finally got to ride Tiana's Bayou Adventure for the first time in August 2024.

And yes, after all that waiting, we loved it. 😂 In fact, we rode it more than once on that first trip. The music, the colors, and the whole atmosphere made it feel like the movie had come to life around us. 🐸💚

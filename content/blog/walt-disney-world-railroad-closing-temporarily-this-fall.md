If you're headed to Magic Kingdom this fall, here's one temporary closure you'll want to know about.

The Walt Disney World Railroad will \*\*temporarily close beginning September 28, 2026\*\* for routine refurbishment.

Disney currently expects the Railroad to reopen sometime in \*\*November 2026\*\*, but an exact reopening date has not been announced.

## Why This Matters for Your Magic Kingdom Day

The Railroad isn't only an attraction. It can also give you a break from walking while moving between areas of Magic Kingdom.

That can be especially helpful with young children, strollers, mobility considerations, or when everyone could simply use a chance to sit down for a little while during a long park day.

If you're visiting during the refurbishment, plan on doing a little more walking than you might on a day when the Railroad is operating.

## Before You Go

Disney currently lists the closure as beginning September 28 with an expected return in November.

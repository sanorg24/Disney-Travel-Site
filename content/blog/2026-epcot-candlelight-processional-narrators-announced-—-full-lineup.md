If your family loves EPCOT during the holidays, one of the season’s biggest traditions now has its 2026 narrator lineup.

Disney has announced the \*\*2026 Candlelight Processional celebrity narrators\*\*, including three new names this year: \*\*Robin Roberts, Jason Kelce and Lacey Chabert\*\*.

Performances take place nightly at \*\*4:45 PM, 6:30 PM and 8:00 PM\*\* at the \*\*America Gardens Theatre\*\* during the EPCOT Festival of the Holidays.

- \*\*Nov. 29–Dec. 1\*\* — Gary Sinise

- \*\*Dec. 24–25\*\* — Jodi Benson
- \*\*Dec. 26–27\*\* — Simu Liu
- \*\*Dec. 28–30\*\* — Eva Longoria

## What Families Should Know

If your family has a favorite narrator, these are good dates to make note of now.

Regular seating for Candlelight Processional is \*\*first-come, first-served\*\*, and Disney says \*\*Candlelight Processional Dining Packages go on sale October 14\*\*. Those packages include a meal at select EPCOT restaurants and guaranteed seating for one performance.

What I like about this update is that it gives families something practical to plan around early. If there’s a narrator your group would really love to see, it may be worth building that EPCOT day around those dates instead of leaving it to chance later.

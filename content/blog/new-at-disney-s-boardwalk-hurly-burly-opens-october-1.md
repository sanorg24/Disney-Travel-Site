Disney's BoardWalk is getting a new place to eat, play and enjoy live entertainment this fall.

\*\*Hurly-Burly opens October 1, 2026\*\*, bringing shareable bites, specialty drinks, daytime games and nighttime live music to the waterfront entertainment district.

During the day, Hurly-Burly welcomes the whole family with games and activities led by an entertainment host. Beginning at \*\*9 PM\*\*, the venue becomes \*\*21+\*\* and shifts into an adults-only evening experience with live musical acts.

## Shareable Bites and Specialty Drinks

![First Look-Roast beef sliders served beside a specialty cocktail from Hurly-Burly at Disney's BoardWalk.](/photos/pasted-image-1788886576166.png)

The menu focuses on shareable appetizers and small plates inspired by Italian-American favorites and classic boardwalk fare.

Options include \*\*Meatballs in Tomato Gravy, Antipasto, Tomato Pie, Oven-Roasted Shrimp, Roast Beef Sliders\*\* and \*\*Mini Apple Cider Doughnuts\*\*.

![Antipasto platter with sliced meats, cheese, vegetables and breadsticks from Hurly-Burly at Disney's BoardWalk.](/photos/20260908-125417.png)

There are also specialty cocktails, wine and beer for adults, along with nonalcoholic choices such as the \*\*Overture in Orange\*\* frozen drink and \*\*Caramel Corn Cold Brew\*\*.

Hurly-Burly is \*\*walk-up only\*\*, so reservations are not required.

## Why We Think Families May Like This One

For families staying in the EPCOT resort area, Hurly-Burly could be an easy addition to a BoardWalk afternoon or evening.

It's also a short walk from EPCOT, which makes it a nice option if you want to step out of the park for a little while without committing to a full sit-down dining reservation.

The walk-up format is especially appealing for families who want something more spontaneous, and the shareable menu could make it easy for everyone to try a few different things.

This also feels like the kind of addition that could work really well on a slower resort day when you want something fun to do without adding another park day or a heavily scheduled meal.

Just remember the biggest planning detail: \*\*families with children will need to visit before 9 PM\*\*, when Hurly-Burly becomes limited to guests ages 21 and older.
